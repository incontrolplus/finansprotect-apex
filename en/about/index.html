About

Financial protection applied to document automation.

To reduce manual work and processing risk in invoices and accounting documents through reliable AI OCR automation built for the Bulgarian business context.

Finans Protect Ltd is a Bulgarian company seated in Sofia, UIC/EIK 206497582, registered in 2021. Its core activity is in legal and financial-protection services, including debt protection, commercial representation and receivables consulting.

With Finans Protect OCR, the company extends into a practical AI product line for document and invoice automation — focused on accounting offices and high-document-volume businesses in Bulgaria.

Position: From invoice to accounting-ready data — faster, more accurate, with human review.
