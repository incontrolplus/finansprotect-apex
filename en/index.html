 FAQ

What is Finans Protect OCR?

An AI OCR service for classifying, extracting, validating and exporting data from invoices and accounting documents.

 FAQ

Who is the service for?

For accounting offices, restaurants, wholesalers, distributors and SMBs with high document volume.

 FAQ

Does it support Microinvest?

The technical pipeline includes exports to Microinvest Sklad XML and Delta Kontirovki.

 FAQ

Does automation replace the accountant?

No. The goal is to reduce manual entry and errors. The review queue enables human verification before final posting or export.

 FAQ

How are personal and company data treated?

The approach is self-hosted/GDPR-aligned, with the option to process data in controlled infrastructure and keep it in-country.

 FAQ

Is the company VAT-registered?

Finans Protect Ltd is currently not VAT-registered. This should be represented accurately in offers and invoicing.
